⚙️ Preferences

About progress calculations

  • Chapter mastered means theory, practice and revision are all marked complete.
  • Question accuracy is correct answers divided by attempted questions across logged practice sets.
  • Mock trend is score divided by maximum score; it does not account for differences in test difficulty.
  • Weak topics are a study aid based on incomplete mastery and recorded practice accuracy, not a prediction of exam results.
